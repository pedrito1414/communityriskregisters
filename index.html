Our goal

Make local risk information easier to find and understand.

Local Resilience Forums bring emergency responders and other organisations together to prepare for emergencies. Their Community Risk Registers describe risks such as flooding, severe weather, disease, and disruption to essential services.

Climate Resilient UK aims to bring these separate publications together, preserve links to the original evidence, and offer different ways to explore the same information. Choose the starting point that works for you.

One shared collection

Two ways in.
The same evidence behind them.

Both tools use the same local risk records, area boundaries, source information, and ranking rules. The dashboard also lets you browse national risks and their draft connections to local records. The information is a maintained snapshot of published sources; it does not update automatically.

What this prototype can tell you

The current local collection covers England and Wales. Some areas contain detailed risk tables; others contain selected topics or summaries. This is not a complete, audited transcription of every register.

These are assessments for wider regions, not predictions for a property or individual. The shared 1–5 rankings help you browse the records; they are not official scores or directly comparable measures of personal risk. Publication dates and assessment methods differ, so always check the original source.

Scotland and Northern Ireland are not included in the local collection. National descriptions and draft local-to-national matches still need checking against their sources.
